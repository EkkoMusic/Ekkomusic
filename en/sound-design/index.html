Sound design brings the real. Music brings the imaginary.

The sound that makes itself forgotten

Sound design is what allows a video to seem real. Good sound must be credible and arrive at exactly the right moment. Even an excellent effect can break a scene if it is poorly placed.

I use professional sound libraries, but rarely the sounds as-is. I search, blend, transform and process different elements to get what the scene requires. I also sometimes record certain elements specifically for the project.

On Decimus Clothes, the video had been sent to me without usable sound recording. I recreated the entire atmosphere in studio: the movements, the interactions with the environment, and details like the sound of water on the actor's face. The work succeeds when the viewer thinks the sound was already there during filming.

When I handle both the musical composition and sound design, I build them together. The music leaves space for the sound design to take its full place when needed, and vice versa.
