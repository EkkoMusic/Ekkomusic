impureza — Le Sanctuaire

Le Sanctuaire · Original music and sound design

  • FormatPresentation
  • ClientLe Sanctuaire
  • Ekko Music's roleOriginal music and sound design

Le Sanctuaire is a metal festival held in Saint-Brieuc, organised by the association L'Antichambre Event. Its second edition took place on 31 October 2026 at the Salle de Robien.

I created the music and sound design for all of the artist intro videos played before each act took the stage. These 3D animated videos featured the guardian of the sanctuary — a monster embodying the lost souls of metal — invoking each of the bands. They were projected onto the giant screen behind the stage, just before the artists came on.

The video published online is the presentation made for Impureza, one of the bands on the lineup.
