Galions, launch trailer

Galions / Sixen · Musical composition, sound design, mixing and mastering

  • FormatTrailer
  • ClientGalions / Sixen
  • Ekko Music's roleMusical composition, sound design, mixing and mastering

For the launch of Galions — the new esport organisation born from the collaboration between Sixen and Team GO — I handled the entire sonic side of the trailer.

I had a great deal of creative freedom on the musical direction and naturally moved toward an orchestral universe. The opening of the piece seeks an atmosphere that is calm, nostalgic and slightly unsettling before gradually building in intensity.

I worked directly with the editor, sharing a common BPM so that the visual and musical transitions could work together almost like in a music video.

An early version was notably influenced by the musical world of The Prince of Egypt. It was later reworked after client feedback to better match the identity sought for Galions.
