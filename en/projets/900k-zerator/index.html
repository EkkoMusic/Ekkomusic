900K, ZeratoR's album

ZeratoR · Composition, production, arrangements, recording, mixing and mastering

  • Project900K 2023
  • ClientZeratoR
  • Ekko Music's roleComposition, production, arrangements, recording, mixing and mastering

Born from a Z Event donation goal, 900K is a 13-track album released in 2023. I supported the project across its entire music production.

To build the album, I composed around thirty sketches and musical ideas, from which ZeratoR selected those he wanted to develop. The result deliberately explores very different worlds: rock, rap, pop, variety and inspirations ranging from the 2000s to more contemporary productions.

The recording notably took place in Montpellier with ZeratoR, Antoine Daniel and Baghera Jones.

The main constraint was time: the entire project was composed, recorded and finalised over a period of just a few months.

Among the tracks, Lyrics remains one of my favourite compositions from the project.
