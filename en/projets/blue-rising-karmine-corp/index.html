Blue Rising

Karmine Corp · Musical composition, sound design, mixing and mastering

  • Format Trailer
  • Client Karmine Corp
  • Director Emash
  • Ekko Music's role Musical composition, sound design, mixing and mastering

Blue Rising is the presentation trailer for the official Karmine Corp TCG, a card game built around the universe and history of the esport organisation.

On this project, I handled the entire musical and sonic side of the trailer: original composition, sound design, mixing and mastering.

Initially, the musical art direction was aimed at something more imaginary, inspired by oriental sounds. After several proposals and exchanges with the team, we ultimately went for a more orchestral and classical approach, while keeping certain more exotic colours.

I worked directly with the edit. We defined a shared tempo so that the transitions and different scenes could be built with the music from the start.

Each section of the trailer needed its own identity while remaining coherent with the whole.

That progression between sections is what I am most satisfied with, along with the final melody that emerged after several proposals.
