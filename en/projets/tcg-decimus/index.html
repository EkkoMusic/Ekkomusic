Decimus TCG

Sixen · Musical composition, sound design, mixing and mastering

  • FormatTrailer
  • ClientSixen
  • Ekko Music's roleMusical composition, sound design, mixing and mastering

For the Decimus TCG trailer, I handled the entire sonic side with a particularly deep approach to film music.

I worked directly with the editor so that the music could follow the trailer's different scenes. The tempo sometimes changes radically from one sequence to another to accompany the image's movements and give more life to the composition.

The beginning of the piece was notably influenced by the musical atmosphere of Interstellar. I also particularly like the section around the stone, carried by melodic voices, as well as the final rise when the two protagonists face each other.

Decimus TCG also exceeded €250,000 in crowdfunding on Kickstarter.
