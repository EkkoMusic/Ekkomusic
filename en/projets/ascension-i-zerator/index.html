L'Ascension I, Trackmania

ZeratoR / ZQSD Productions · Musical composition, mixing and mastering

  • EventL'Ascension 2023, Trackmania
  • ClientZeratoR / ZQSD Productions
  • Ekko Music's roleMusical composition, mixing and mastering

For the first edition of L'Ascension on Trackmania, I built the event's musical identity alongside ZQSD Productions. Rather than following the usual esport codes, I proposed a direction inspired by the concept of Ascension itself: progressing, following a winding road, reaching the summit. That led me toward a slightly overdriven guitar with bends that evoked the curves of a mountain road.

I composed several pieces and variations for the different moments of the event: player entrances, waiting music, transitions and victory. The whole aimed to balance that personal direction with the immediate energy required by a large-scale esport event.

The most memorable moment remains the finale opening at the Sud de France Arena in Montpellier. Hearing that music in a hall packed with thousands of spectators gave the studio work an entirely different dimension.
