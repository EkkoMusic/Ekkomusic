L'Ascension II, Fortnite

ZeratoR / ZQSD Productions · Music by Ekko Music, mixing and mastering

  • EventL'Ascension II, Fortnite
  • ClientZeratoR / ZQSD Productions
  • Ekko Music's roleMusic, mixing and mastering

For this second edition of L'Ascension dedicated to Fortnite, I started from scratch with a more electronic, faster and more percussive direction than the first edition. I also integrated certain characteristic Fortnite sounds directly into the compositions. The Battle Bus beep appears, for example, as a subtle reference aimed at players.

I composed the various pieces of music needed for the live event: player entrances, waiting, victory and interludes. A significant part of the work involved designing tracks capable of looping with different variations, so the control team could adapt their duration live without causing cuts.

I worked in coordination with the production team and with Meteora at ZQSD for the musical art direction of the project.
