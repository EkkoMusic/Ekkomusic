L'Ascension II, Fortnite

ZeratoR / ZQSD Productions · Musical composition, mixing and mastering

  • EventL'Ascension II, Fortnite
  • ClientZeratoR / ZQSD Productions
  • Ekko Music's roleMusical composition, mixing and mastering

For this second edition of L'Ascension — dedicated to Fortnite — I started from scratch with a direction more electronic, fast and percussive than the first edition. I also embedded characteristic Fortnite sounds directly into the compositions. The Battle Bus beep appears as a subtle reference aimed at players.

I composed the various tracks needed for the live show: player entrances, waiting music, victory themes and interludes. A significant part of the work was designing pieces that could loop with different variations, allowing the production crew to adjust their length in real time without any jarring cuts.

I worked closely with the production team and with Meteora on the ZQSD side for the musical art direction of the project.
