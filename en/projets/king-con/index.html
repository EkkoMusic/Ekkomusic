King Con, Super Smash Bros. Ultimate

SINJ Crew · Musical composition, sound design, mixing and mastering

  • EventKing Con 2024
  • ClientSINJ Crew
  • Ekko Music's roleMusical composition, sound design, mixing and mastering

For King Con, the Super Smash Bros. Ultimate tournament organised in Paris, I first composed the music for the event's trailer.

The team particularly appreciated this first composition, so they then asked me to develop a new complete version intended to be broadcast during the event itself.

I handled the entire sonic side, from the composition to the sound design, through to mixing and mastering.

I also integrated a musical reference from Super Smash Bros. Ultimate into the piece, slipped into the second part of the composition as a nod to the game's universe.
