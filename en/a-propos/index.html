Background

Ekko Music is me, Pierre Sanges. I'm a music producer, composer and sound designer, specialising in music for image, trailer, gaming, esport and live events.

I started out as a stage musician. Music for image came quite naturally, bringing together all my passions: music, film, animation, video games and pop culture. My work today is about creating a sonic identity that genuinely serves a project, rather than simply placing music on top of it.

In the end, it's quite simply how life led me here.

My approach to composition

I work on original musical composition, sound design, mixing, mastering and trailer music. I specialise in orchestral compositions, but I don't like to be confined to a single genre. Depending on the project, I'll explore electronic, industrial, more modern or entirely different sounds.

Every project starts with a conversation. I like to gather several references to understand the direction being sought. Those references serve as a starting point, not a template to reproduce.

I don't use generative AI to replace the composition work. What interests me in this craft is understanding a project, searching for ideas and building something genuinely tailored.

Influences

My influences come from film music. Hans Zimmer and Ludwig Göransson inspire me in particular through their way of blending orchestration with contemporary sounds. My references also come from video games and studios like Riot Games, who build very strong musical universes by mixing different genres.

Christopher Nolan's cinema, manga, animation and pop culture are all part of what feeds my approach to composition.

Work with Ekko Music

I work from France with productions, creators, studios and organisations looking for original music or a genuine sonic direction. If you have an idea, a few references or simply a universe in mind, we can start by talking it through.
