Background

Ekko Music is me, Pierre Sanges. I am a music producer, composer and sound designer, specialising in film music, trailers, gaming, esport and live events.

I started as a live musician. Film music came quite naturally by bringing together all my passions: music, cinema, animation, video games and pop culture. My work today consists of creating a sonic identity that truly accompanies a project, rather than simply laying music on top of it.

It is quite simply, in the end, how life led me here.

How I compose

I work on original musical composition, sound design, mixing, mastering and trailer music. I am fairly specialised in orchestral compositions, but I don't like to stay confined to a single genre. Depending on the project, I can explore electronic, industrial, more modern or completely different sounds.

Every project begins with an exchange. I like to have several references to understand the direction sought. These references serve as a starting point, not a model to reproduce.

I do not use generative AI to replace the composition work. What interests me in this profession is understanding a project, searching for ideas and building something truly personalised.

Influences

My influences come from film music. Hans Zimmer and Ludwig Göransson inspire me particularly in their way of blending orchestration with contemporary sounds. My references also come from video games and studios like Riot Games, which create very strong musical universes by mixing different genres.

Christopher Nolan's cinema, manga, animation and pop culture are part of what feeds my way of composing.

Working with Ekko Music

I work from France with productions, creators, studios and organisations looking for original music or a true sonic direction. If you have an idea, some references or simply a universe, we can start by discussing it.
