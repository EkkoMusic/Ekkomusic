Composing for the image

For me, trailer music should not simply dress the image. It should be a full part of the trailer itself — tell a story, create tension, or give impact to a precise moment.

When possible, I like to work early with the editor. We can define a starting tempo together so that certain actions or transitions fall exactly with the music. I can also receive an already-edited trailer and compose directly from the image.

My speciality remains hybrid orchestral music, blending orchestral elements with electronic or industrial sounds. If a project has a particular cultural identity, I take the time to understand the scales, modes and timbres that truly build that identity.

I can handle the entire sonic side of a trailer: composition, sound design, mixing and mastering. This allows for a coherent sonic direction with one person who understands the whole universe. Library music suits some projects, but an original composition goes much further when a project wants to tell a precise story.
