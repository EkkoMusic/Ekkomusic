An event doesn't need a single piece of music. It needs an entire sonic universe.

Building a sonic universe

Composing for an esport event means building a complete sonic universe around a main theme. Player entrances, victory, defeat, transitions, moments of tension: each situation needs its own music, but all of them must belong to the same universe.

On L'Ascension Fortnite, I discretely integrated the sound of the Fortnite battle bus into the player entrance music. Someone who doesn't know the game simply hears it as an element of the composition. Someone who plays Fortnite recognises the reference immediately. That's the kind of detail that shows the music was designed for this event and this community.

Some pieces of music also need to loop naturally for several minutes. Others are designed for very precise moments. I can handle the composition, mixing and mastering, ideally exchanging with the person in charge of sound on-site to adapt certain production choices.
