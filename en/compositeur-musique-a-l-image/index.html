It's not just about synchronising music with the image. You have to synchronise the emotions too.

An extra dimension

Film music isn't simply laying a background sound over a video. If the image is already telling something, the music can go searching for something else: a hidden emotion, a tension, something the image alone cannot show.

When I discover a video, one of the first things I look for is its tempo. Not just its BPM, but what I would call the tempo of the mood: the feeling of speed or space the video conveys. Then comes the story, and above all when the emotions need to appear. Sometimes the music must arrive before the emotion. Sometimes during. Sometimes after.

I can work from a storyboard, an animatic or a nearly complete edit. The earlier I step in, the more it is possible to think of the image and the music as a whole. I can also receive a finalised video and compose directly on it.

I work mainly on films, trailers, animation, advertising and film and video. Among my projects: Decimus TCG, Galions 2026, King Con, Blue Rising and the editions of L'Ascension.
